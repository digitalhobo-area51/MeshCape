# Cape Town region & coverage

Cape Town's terrain is a gift and a curse for LoRa: Table Mountain, Lion's Head, Tygerberg and the Helderberg give superb high sites, but the same mountains block line of sight between the Atlantic seaboard, the City Bowl, the southern suburbs and the northern suburbs. A useful mesh needs repeaters that can "see" around the mountain.
